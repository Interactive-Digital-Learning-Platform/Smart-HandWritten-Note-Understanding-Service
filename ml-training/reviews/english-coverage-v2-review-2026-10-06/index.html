How to label
  • Complete: the required ideas are adequately and correctly explained or demonstrated. Concise notes can be complete.
  • Partial: meaningful relevant explanation is present, but a required idea is absent or there is an unresolved substantive error.
  • Missing: there is no meaningful explanation of this target. A bare heading or a neighbouring topic does not count.
  • Unclear / checklist issue: the target, checklist or example is ambiguous. Explain the issue in the comment.
