ICT concept criteria review

Review these 10 proposed concepts and 30 synthetic English examples. No ICT model has been trained. This is a development review, not an accuracy evaluation.

The criteria are proposed from the supplied textbooks and teacher guides. Approve them or request changes. A concept applies only when it belongs in the selected note's lesson or question; an absent out-of-scope concept must not be called missing.

Complete: all required points are correctly explained. Partial: meaningful correct coverage with an important omission or error. Missing: no meaningful correct coverage; a heading alone is insufficient. Choose Unsure if the criterion or answer needs discussion.
